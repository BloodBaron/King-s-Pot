Ready. The first player to establish a crown becomes King.
Normal roll: 1 = out, 3 = pay and reroll, 2/4/5/6 can take the throne. Higher beats the crown, ties cause a duel, and 2 beats a crown of 6.

King's turn: pass, or pay to try to match the crown. A 1 eliminates the King and a 3 forces another paid roll.

Fealty: when elimination leaves 2 or more players standing, the eliminated player clicks one surviving player on the wheel to swear fealty. If that player wins, the eliminated player is refunded everything they paid into the game, bringing their final net back to neutral. The refund comes from the final pot before the winner takes the remainder. If only one player remains, Fealty is skipped.
